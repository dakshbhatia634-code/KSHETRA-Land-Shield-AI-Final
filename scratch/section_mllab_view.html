📐 Technical Methodology & Model Disclosure (Density Clustering Engine) ▼

Internal Formulation: Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise (DBSCAN) operating over standardized 4-dimensional vectors ($\text{lat}, \text{lon}, \text{veg}\%, \text{built}\%$) with hyperspherical neighborhood radius $\epsilon = 0.45$ and density threshold $MinPts = 6$.

Anomaly Identification: Points not density-reachable from any core cluster boundary receive noise designation (label -1), reliably flagging real earth phenomena such as quarry excavations, industrial runoff, and coastal salt pans.

📐 Technical Methodology & Model Disclosure (Instance-Based Distance Metric) ▼

Distance Formula: Standardized multi-dimensional Euclidean metric $d(p, q) = \sqrt{\sum \frac{(p_i - q_i)^2}{\sigma_i^2}}$ over an 8-dimensional optical and land-cover feature space.
