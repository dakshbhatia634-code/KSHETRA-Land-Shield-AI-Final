⚠️ Subterranean Soil Chemistry

What is missing: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), and pH levels. Spaceborne satellites cannot measure subterranean chemistry.

Source required: Physical core test at an ICAR / Krishi Vigyan Kendra testing lab.

⚠️ Borehole Load-Bearing Capacity

What is missing: Soil safe bearing capacity (SBC) for foundation engineering.

Source required: Certified geotechnical engineer standard penetration test (SPT).

⚠️ Cadastral Deeds & Ownership

What is missing: Legal land title, mortgages, disputes, and non-encumbrance status.

Source required: Certified Jamabandi / Bhulekh Record of Rights from the local Sub-Registrar / Tehsildar.
